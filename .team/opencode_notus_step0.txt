## Uncertainties / things you should decide on
1. **The new reader does not yet read Notus digits correctly.** `_segment_digit` uses `data/scoreboard_digits.npz`, which appears to be Adrian glyphs. Live tests: A1 202s read home `0` as `1` and away `4` as unreadable; A 2435s read home `19` as `18` and away `15` unreadable; A 187s read home `5` and away `2` unreadable. I did not tune this because the Golden Rules forbid it without a vet. Likely fix options for the Vetter: use the seven-segment `_slot_digit` path, or build Notus templates.
2. **`_warm_mask` misses the Notus A clock.** At A 187s only 189 clock pixels pass `_warm_mask` (A1's clock is bright). The measure script uses a measurement-only looser yellow mask (H 8–40, S≥45, V≥140) to locate boxes; the reader still uses `_warm_mask` as the plan requires, so Notus A clock will likely read empty until that is addressed.
3. **Clock text parsing:** the colon is segmented as a glyph and becomes `?` (A1 202s raw group `63?2`), so `_clock_text` returns `None`. Also under one minute the board shows `SS.T` (e.g. `45.7`, seen at A_07), which does not fit the M:SS logic.
4. **Clock min/sec boxes are nominal M:SS only.** In `SS.T` crops (e.g. `notus_A_07_2810s.png`) those magenta boxes do not align; the orange `clock_field` is the authoritative box and does cover both formats.
5. The board is confirmed as a fixed overlay in the crops. HOME is PATRIOTS, away is GUEST; single-digit scores sit in the ones slot with a blank tens slot, and the crops reflect that.
