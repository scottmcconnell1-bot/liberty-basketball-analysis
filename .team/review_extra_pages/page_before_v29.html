The rules

  1. On the ball: about 4 ft off his man, toward the middle of the free-throw line. He is the only defender allowed outside the 3-point line.
  2. Everyone else builds a triangle with the ball and his man, and stays inside the 3-point line.
  3. One pass away: about 40% of the way to the ball, 4 ft off the passing lane toward the basket.
  4. Two passes away: about halfway to the ball, 4 to 5 ft toward the basket.
  5. Three passes away (far side): helps in the middle of the lane, about 10 ft out from the baseline.
  6. Post defenders: stay tight, a small step to the ball side.
  7. Ball in the post: everyone sinks toward the ball.
  8. Wing beaten baseline — Fill, Sink, Close: the low-post defender stops the ball (close); the high-post defender drops to the low post (sink); the point's defender fills to the high post (fill); the far-wing defender sinks to the rim; the beaten defender recovers toward the point. Anything else is normal man.
