### Choices the spec left open

- Stillness links a player by nearest centre on frames 5 apart. The speed used is the 25-frame step, divided by that box height and by `25/fps`. Each player contributes the median of their speeds; the moment uses the median of those. No pair means not still.
- A ball-holder is the nearest still-frame box. There is no extra match radius.
- Candidates run in `(x, y, w, h)` order. The first ellipse with exactly one candidate's feet wins. An ellipse with 0 or 2+ feet does not cancel a later candidate.
- The major axis is OpenCV's full axis (the longer side of `fitEllipse`), because a 6 ft radius is about two player heights across.
- Point-to-ellipse distance is the sampled Euclidean distance to `(a cos t, b sin t)` rotated by the OpenCV angle. The sample count is `ceil(2π max(a, b))`, about 1 px along the longer semi-axis. The sign is negative inside the ellipse. The inlier test uses the absolute value. Points are scored 2048 at a time.
- Hough scale is frame width/1280. The min-length and max-gap floors are the ones in `extract_lines` (12 and 4). The erase stroke is the yaml `merge_max_offset_px`.
- The debug image draws the paint mask before straight segments are removed.
- A free-throw hit clears `reason`. An orange `ft_circle_not_found` also sets `shooter` and `shooter_reason`, and leaves the trace shooter box.
- `free_throw` and `ft_reason` are written when the step runs. `--ft-off`, or a retrace without both `--video` and `--gym`, does not add them.
- The free-throw step always merges and drops referees with the spec 39 rules. `--fixes-off` still changes only the trace.
- The live pass opens the video again for still frames.
- `region_for` takes the frame width and height so the square can be clipped.
- A missing frame is `no_frame`. A frame that will not read is `frame_unreadable`. The trace is left as it was.
- In the measure, `false` counts matched non-FT tags that were flagged. The flagged list includes every flagged moment; the event type is empty when that moment was not in the match.
