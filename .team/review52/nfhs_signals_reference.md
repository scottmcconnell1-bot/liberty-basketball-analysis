# Official NFHS basketball signals (2024-25 chart, assets.nfhs.org; copy: nfhs_signal_chart_2024-25.pdf)
Read from the chart pictures by Claude Code 2026-11-14. Supersedes the signal list in prompt_team_refsignals53.txt (that list was from memory; the "3 fingers" and "waved off = arms across body" wording there was wrong or loose).

Starting/stopping clock
1 Start clock: arm raised open hand, chopped down (downward arc)
2 Stop clock: one arm straight up, open hand
3 Stop clock for jump/held ball: arm up, then both thumbs up (arms forward/up)
4 Stop clock for foul: one arm straight up, CLOSED FIST
5 Stop clock for foul (optional bird dog): fist up + other arm pointing at the fouler
Information
6 Directional signal: arm extended sideways, flat hand, pointing direction
7 Throw-in / FT designated spot / violation: pointing down at the spot
8 Move along endline on throw-in: hand across, sweeping past shoulder
9 Visible counts: arm extended to side, swinging count
10 Beckoning substitutes: open hand waving in
11 60-second time-out: both arms extended out to sides
12 30-second time-out: both hands touching shoulders
13 Not closely guarded: both arms out to sides
14 Tipped ball: one hand tapping fingertips above the other above head
15 Flopping: two hands raised, pushing back motion
Shooting/scoring
16 No score: both arms crossing/scissoring in front at waist-chest height, out to sides (waved off)
17 Goal counts: arm up then dropped down (pull-down)
18 Points scored: arm out to side with 1 or 2 fingers
19 3-point attempt: one arm raised with three fingers extended; 3-point made / attempt score: BOTH arms straight up
Free throws
20 Signal free throw: finger(s) up showing number of free throws (1 or 2, bonus shown in circle)
Violations
21 Delayed violation (withheld whistle): arm extended to side, fist
22 Traveling: rotating fists (rolling arms in front of chest)
23 Illegal dribble: patting motion / up-down hands
24 Palming/carry: hand turning over
25 Backcourt / first to touch from out of bounds: arm swinging over/behind
26 3-second: arm out with three fingers (count) / swing
27 5-second: one open hand, five fingers
28 10-second: both open hands, ten fingers
29 Excessive swinging elbows: elbow swing
30 Kicking: leg kick motion
Fouls (after the stop-clock fist, the official reports the type)
31 Illegal use of hands: strike wrist
32 Hand check: arm extended, hand flat up (stop-like)
33 Holding: grasp wrist
34 Blocking: both hands on hips
35 Pushing/charging: two palms pushing forward
36 Player/team control: fist behind head
37 Intentional foul: crossed fists above head
38 Double foul: both arms out to sides, fists
39 Technical foul: T with hands

Key cues for shot decisions (proposal for the team): fouled attempt = stop-clock FIST (4/5) within a few seconds of the shot; waved-off basket / no score = 16; violation on the shot (e.g. travel 22) = stop-clock open hand (2) then violation signal; and-one = goal counts (17) or points signal plus foul fist; 3-pt attempt/made = 19; dead ball / clock stopped = 2/4/11/12; restart = 1.
