About player labels (Pos 0, Pos 3, …)

Automated analysis does not read jersey numbers from the video. Pos 0–9 are court-area clusters — the AI groups detections by where people appear on the camera view. They are not roster numbers, and they are not in jersey order. Minutes = time someone was detected in that court area (any player who stood there). To get real names/stats, map events to your roster manually in Film Tool or import a roster under Teams.
