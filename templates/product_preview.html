Typical coach flow

This is the intended rhythm of the finished product, from preparation through review and follow-up.

1. Plan

Set schedule, game context, and practice work without leaving the platform.

2. Upload

Bring in film, launch analysis, and track progress in one place.

3. Review

Accept, correct, or reject AI-generated events before they become trusted stats.

4. Teach

Turn reviewed clips and possessions into player-development work and practice plans.

5. Scout

Keep opponent prep, playbook notes, and team messaging in the same operating system.
