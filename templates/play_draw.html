Action

Man shell. Set each offensive player, then press Move them and drag him the way he goes. Click a player and the X's shift to the ball. Drag an X and he keeps that spot relative to the ball. On the ball we take the high shoulder and force the sideline and the baseline. One pass away is on the line. Two passes away is in the gap. Three passes away is off the inside shoulder, help side, in front of the lob. It stays ball-you-man on a cut to the basket. A cut across the lane is chest-to-chest. On a post exchange the screener's defender hedges and the cutter's defender goes over. If the screen beats him, they switch and the hedge fronts the cutter. High posts are half-denied. At the free-throw line and below, posts are fronted. Every screen is hedged and gone over. This picture files under Defense / Man.
